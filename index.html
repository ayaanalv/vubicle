Plain answers

Where do my files go?

They are read and stored in your browser. To answer a question, only the passages that match it are sent to Google Gemini, or to Groq when Gemini is busy. In the background, passages also go to Google once so search can find them by meaning. Photos are sent whole, once, so they can be read. The privacy page has the details.

Can Google or Groq see my notes?

They see the passages sent with each request, and any photos you add. Free tiers may use that text to improve their services, so do not upload anything confidential.

Does it cost anything?

No. Vubicle runs on free tiers. When they are busy, you wait in a short line with a countdown instead of seeing an error.

Do I need an account?

No. Your cubicles live in this browser. Export one to a file to move it to another computer or keep a backup.

Which files work?

PDF, PowerPoint (.pptx), Word (.docx), typed notes and photos of notes, whiteboards or textbook pages. Photos are read by the AI, so the whole photo is sent once. Save old .ppt and .doc files in the newer format first. Scanned PDFs need text recognition before they can be read.

What if my notes do not cover it?

Vubicle says so. It answers from your material, not from what the model happens to know.
