From a pile of files
to one desk

Make a cubicle for each course. Everything you add to it becomes something you can search, question and quiz yourself on.

  1. Drop

    PDFs, PowerPoint, Word files and typed notes. They are read in your browser, page by page.

  2. Index

    Each page is cut into short passages and indexed, so the right one turns up for every question.

  3. Study

    Chat, flashcards, quizzes and a tutor work from those passages and cite the slide or page.

Plain answers

Where do my files go?

They are read and stored in your browser. To answer a question, only the passages that match it are sent to Google Gemini, or to Groq when Gemini is busy.

Can Google or Groq see my notes?

They see the passages sent with each request. Free tiers may use that text to improve their services, so do not upload anything confidential.

Does it cost anything?

No. Vubicle runs on free tiers. When they are busy, you wait in a short line with a countdown instead of seeing an error.

Do I need an account?

No. Your cubicles live in this browser. Export one to a file to move it to another computer or keep a backup.

Which files work?

PDF, PowerPoint (.pptx), Word (.docx) and typed notes. Save old .ppt and .doc files in the newer format first. Scanned PDFs need text recognition before they can be read.

What if my notes do not cover it?

Vubicle says so. It answers from your material, not from what the model happens to know.
