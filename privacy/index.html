Privacy

Vubicle has no accounts and keeps no copy of your files on a server. Your course material lives in your browser. Some of its text goes to Google Gemini or Groq when an AI tool needs it, as set out below.

Last updated November 4, 2026

What stays in your browser

Your files, the passages cut from them, and everything you make (flashcards, quizzes, chats, briefs, concept maps and deadlines) are saved in this browser's storage on this device. They are not uploaded anywhere to be stored. Vubicle also keeps a few settings in the browser, such as your theme and the last tool you used.

A backup is a file you download yourself. Vubicle does not keep a copy of it.

What is sent to the AI

  • When you use an AI tool, Vubicle sends the passages that tool picked from your files, plus your question or answer when there is one. For a question, that is a handful of matching passages. For a brief, a concept map or a quiz with no topic, it is passages sampled across a file or the course.
  • For smart search, the text of every passage is sent once, in the background, to Google's embedding service. It turns each passage into numbers that help search find passages by meaning. If this is unavailable, Vubicle uses keyword search, which runs entirely in your browser.
  • Photos are sent whole, once, when you add them, so the AI can write out their text. After that, only the text is used.

Requests go through Vubicle's server to Google Gemini, or to Groq when Gemini is busy. The server passes them on and does not store them.

What Google and Groq do with it

Vubicle uses the free tier of the Gemini API. Under the Gemini API terms, Google uses what is sent on the free tier to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Groq's handling of API data is set out in its services agreement.

So do not add anything confidential: personal information, unpublished research, or material you are not allowed to share.

What the server keeps

  • A request count. To enforce the hourly limit on AI requests, the server stores a count under a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address. It is deleted after the hour. Your IP address itself is not stored.
  • Short-lived logs. Each AI request logs which tool was used, which provider answered and how long it took. The logs never include your text, and the host deletes them within a day.

The site is hosted on Vercel, which, like any web host, sees the IP address of each visit. Vubicle has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

To delete one course, open its menu in the workspace and choose Delete this cubicle. To delete everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings. Text already sent to Google or Groq is handled under their terms above and cannot be recalled by Vubicle.
